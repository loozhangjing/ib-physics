# Quantities

| Symbol          | Quantity                 | Meaning                                    | S.I. Unit       |
| --------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| $T$             | period                   | time taken to complete one full revolution | $\pu{s}$        |
| $f$             | frequency                | number of full revolutions per second      | $\pu{s^-1}$     |
| $r$             | radius                   |                                            | $\pu{m}$        |
| $v$             | speed                    |                                            | $\pu{m s^-1}$   |
| $\Delta \theta$ | **angular displacement** | angle                                      | $\pu{rad}$      |
| $\omega$        | **angular speed**        | rate of change of angle                    | $\pu{rad s^-1}$ |

![[circular motion quantities.png|379]]

Using common sense,
$$\begin{aligned}
v &= \dfrac{2 \pi r}{T}\\
\\
\omega &= \dfrac{\Delta \theta}{\Delta t}\\
\end{aligned}$$
and of course
$$f = \dfrac{1}{T}$$
For a complete revolution, $\Delta \theta = 2 \pi$ and $\Delta t = T$, therefore
$$\omega = \dfrac{2 \pi}{T}$$
Since $v = r \cdot \dfrac{2 \pi}{T}$,
$$v = r \omega$$
