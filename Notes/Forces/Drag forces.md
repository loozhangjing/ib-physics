> [!quote] Definition
> Forces that oppose the motion of a body through a fluid.

> [!info]-
> - a fluid can be a gas or a liquid
> - e.g. air resistance force
# Stoke's law
For a **spherical** body falling through a fluid, the drag force is given by **Stoke's law**:
$$F_D = 6 \pi \eta rv$$
where:
- $\eta$ is the viscosity of the fluid
- $r$ is the radius of the spherical body
- $v$ is the velocity of the body
