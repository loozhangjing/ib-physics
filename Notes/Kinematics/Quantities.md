## Velocity
When velocity is constant, it is
$$v = \dfrac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}$$
When velocity is *not* constant, the **average velocity** is:
$$\bar v = \dfrac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}$$
while the **instantaneous velocity** is:
$$v = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0}\dfrac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}$$
Instantaneous velocity is the gradient of a tangent to a displacement-time curve.
## Speed or average speed?
Speed is the magnitude of (instantaneous) velocity, while *average* speed is the total distance travelled divided by the time taken.
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Speed} &= |v|\\
\text{Average speed} &= \dfrac{\text{Total distance}}{t}
\end{aligned}$$
