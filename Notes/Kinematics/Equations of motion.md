# Derivations
## 1: No $s$
$$\begin{aligned}
a &= \dfrac{v - u}{t}\\
v &= u + at
\end{aligned}$$
## 2: No $v$
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Displacement} &= \text{Average velocity} \cdot \text{Time}\\
s &= (\dfrac{v + u}{2}) \cdot t\\
\text{Substitute } v = u + at,\\
s &= (\dfrac{u + at + u}{2}) \cdot t\\
s &= (\dfrac{2u + at}{2}) \cdot t\\
s &= \dfrac{2ut + at^2}{2}\\
s &= ut + \dfrac{1}{2}at^2
\end{aligned}$$
## 3: No $t$
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Displacement} &= \text{Average velocity} \cdot \text{Time}\\
s &= (\dfrac{v + u}{2}) \cdot t\\
\text{Substitute } t = \dfrac{v - u}{a},\\
s &= (\dfrac{v + u}{2})(\dfrac{v - u}{a})\\
s &= \dfrac{(v + u)(v - u)}{2a}\\
s &= \dfrac{v^2 - u^2}{2a}\\
2as &= v^2 - u^2\\
v^2 &= u^2 + 2as
\end{aligned}$$
