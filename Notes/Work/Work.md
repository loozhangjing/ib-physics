Work is force multiplied by displacement, where:
- the force is in the exact same or exact opposite direction of the displacement
- work is a scalar quantity
# Work and kinetic energy
$$\begin{aligned}
v^2 &= u^2 + 2as\\
\text{Substitute } a &= \dfrac{F}{m},\\
v^2 &= u^2 + 2(\dfrac{F}{m})s\\
v^2 - u^2 &= \dfrac{2Fs}{m}\\
\dfrac{1}{2}m(v^2 - u^2) &= Fs\\
Fs &= \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}mu^2\\
\\
W &= \Delta K_E\\
\text{Work done} &= \text{Change in kinetic energy}
\end{aligned}$$
