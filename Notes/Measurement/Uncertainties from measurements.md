# Measuring instruments
## Analogue measuring instruments
The uncertainty in the reading is **half of the smallest division**.
### Example
The smallest division in the ruler below is $\pu{0.1 cm}$, so the uncertainty is $\pm \pu{0.05 cm}$.
![[uncertainty of a ruler.png|363]]
## Digital measuring instruments
The uncertainty in the reading is its **smallest digit**.
### Example
If the measurement of a digital balance is $\pu{20.5 g}$, its uncertainty is $\pm \pu{0.1 g}$.
# Repeat measurements
The uncertainty is half of the range of the measured values.
$$\text{Uncertainty} = \dfrac{\text{Largest value} - \text{Smallest value}}{2}$$
