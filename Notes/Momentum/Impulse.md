There is no such thing as "impulsive force" in IBDP Physics, but there is "average force".
$$F_{avg} = \dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$$
When the force is not constant throughout time $t$, there will naturally be a minimum and maximum force. Note that $\dfrac{\Delta p} {\Delta t}$ gives the average force, **not the maximum force**.
