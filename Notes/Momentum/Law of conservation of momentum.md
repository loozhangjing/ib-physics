>[!quote] Definition
>The momentum of a system is conserved when the net force on it is zero..

>[!info]
>A *conserved* quantity is one that does not change.
## Derivation
The net force on a system is $F_\text{net} = \dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$.
If $F_\text{net} = 0$, then $\Delta p = 0$. Therefore,
$$\begin{aligned}
p_\text{final} - p_\text{initial} &= 0 \\
mv - mu &= 0\\
mv &= mu
\end{aligned}$$
>[!warning]
> $mu$ refers to the *total* initial momentum of the system. In the case where there are two bodies, $mu = m_1u_1 + m_2u_2$.
# Elastic and inelastic collisions
- in an elastic collision, both $\Delta p$ and kinetic energy, $E_K$ are conserved
- in an inelastic collision, only $\Delta p$ is conserved
