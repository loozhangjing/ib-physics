# Question
A sphere of density $\rho$ and radius $R$ rests on the bottom of a tank of water. The buoyant force on the sphere is $F_1$.

A sphere of density $2\rho$ and radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$ is at the bottom of the same tank. The buoyant force on the second sphere is $F_2$.

What is $\dfrac{F_1}{F_2}$?
# Answer
Apply Archimedes' Principle:
$\text{Buoyant force, } F_B = \text{Weight of displaced fluid, } W$


$$\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{F_1}{F_2} &= \dfrac{W_1}{W_2}
\end{aligned}$$
