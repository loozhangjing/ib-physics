**Two blocks of mass 4.0 and 6.0 kg are joined by a string and rest on a frictionless horizontal table. A force of 100 N is applied horizontally on one of the blocks. We want to find the acceleration of each block and the tension of the string joining the two blocks.**

The two blocks and the string can be considered as one body, which means that the acceleration of both blocks is the same.
$$\begin{aligned}
a &= \dfrac{F}{m}\\
&= \dfrac{\pu{100 N}}{\pu{(4 + 6) kg}}\\
&= \pu{10 m s^-2}
\end{aligned}$$
