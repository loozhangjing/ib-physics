# Question
A basketball is launched at an angle and undergoes projectile motion. Air resistance is negligible. Which of the following is correct for the direction of its velocity vector and its acceleration vector at the highest point of its trajectory?
*source: RevisionVillage (multiple-choice questions)*
# Solution
Direction of velocity vector: horizontal
Direction of acceleration vector: downward
## Explanation
At the top of its trajectory, the basketball's vertical velocity is zero. The horizontal velocity, however but the horizontal component of the velocity will maintain the same value throughout the motion, therefore velocity will be horizontal.

Acceleration due to gravity is always acting and acts downward.
